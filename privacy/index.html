Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 14, 2026

BrochureDesk is operated by Silverfold LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("BrochureDesk," "we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use brochuredesk.com, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. Questions or requests go to support@brochuredesk.com.

#What you give us

  • Your account. Your email and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we can't see them.
  • Your details, if you add them. Name, email, cell number and headshot for your pieces.
  • Your brand kit. Brokerage logo, colors, fonts and the contact info you want on your pieces.
  • Each listing. The listing link you paste, or the photos and details you enter yourself (address, beds, baths, square feet, year built, stories, neighborhood, description), a floor plan if you add one, Open House dates and times, and any notes.
  • Printer and mail vendor connections, if you use them. You sign in to that company yourself. We keep the signed-in session, encrypted, so we can place your file in your cart. We never see or keep your password there. You can disconnect any time.
  • Payments. Stripe takes your card on its own checkout page. We never see or store card numbers. We keep a record of each payment: the amount, what you bought, the date and Stripe's reference number.
  • Support emails you send to support@brochuredesk.com.

#What we look up for you

When you paste a listing link, we read that listing's public page to pull its photos, facts, description and public documents like a floor plan. We read one listing per paste. Some listing sites we don't read at all; for those, we use the address in the link to find the same listing on a site we do use.

#How we use it

We use your information to make, fix, deliver and store your pieces, to place files with your printer or mail vendor when you ask us to, to take payment, to answer support emails, to prevent fraud and refund abuse, and to keep the site working. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

#AI writing

We use AI models from Anthropic (Claude) to write and check the copy on your pieces and to read your listing photos. To do that, we send them the listing facts, description and photos. We don't send your payment details or printer sign-ins.

#Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run BrochureDesk, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and server functions
  • Anthropic: writing and checking copy, reading photos
  • Stripe: payments
  • Browserbase and our own print server hosted on DigitalOcean: making print files and uploading them to your printer or mail vendor
  • Pangram: a check that copy reads like a person wrote it
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website
  • PostHog: measures how people use the site and records sessions (see "On your device")
  • Google Fonts: loads fonts on the website, which means Google receives your browser's IP address
  • Microsoft 365: our support email
  • Your printer or mail vendor, when you connect one and ask us to upload

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or our users' safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business (the new owner would be bound by this policy).

The pieces we make carry your name and your brokerage's details, because that's what they're for. Anyone who receives a postcard or picks up a flyer will see what's printed on it.

#On your device

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember the package you're working on, a link you pasted, and your settings. If you choose "Use this browser only," your packages stay in that browser and aren't saved to your account.

We use PostHog to see how people use the site: which pages and designs they look at, how long steps take, and where they get stuck. PostHog sets its own cookie or browser storage for this and records sessions so we can see what went wrong when something breaks. Anything you type, like names, emails and phone numbers, is masked in those recordings, and payment fields are never recorded at all (card entry happens on Stripe's page anyway). If your browser blocks cookies or sends a "Do Not Track" signal, PostHog doesn't track or record you.

We don't use advertising cookies, we don't track you across other sites, and we never sell what we learn. One link on the site uses a simple counter that records only that the link was clicked, with no personal information.

#How long we keep it

  • Your orders. Each full package (photos, copy, fact sheet, floor plan, layout and your choices) is kept for at least 2 years after the order. That's what makes the Just Sold postcard, Open House updates and the free brokerage rebrand possible. Orders show in your account for 60 days; after that they're hidden but we can restore them.
  • Uploaded photos are kept in your own private folder in our storage. The links to them work for about a day, and new ones are made when you open your account, so an old link can't be used to see your photos.
  • Site-use data and session recordings from PostHog are kept for as long as PostHog's plan keeps them, then deleted.
  • Payment records are kept as long as we need them for taxes and accounting.
  • Printer and mail vendor sessions are kept until you disconnect or the session expires.

If you'd rather we delete something sooner, ask us (see below). Some records, like payment records, we may need to keep to meet legal or tax requirements.

#Your choices

You can update your details and brand kit in your account. You can also email support@brochuredesk.com to:

  • get a copy of your information,
  • correct it,
  • delete your account, your listings or your files,
  • disconnect a printer or mail vendor.

We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act.

#Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, private storage folders, encrypted printer sessions and limited access. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

#Children

BrochureDesk is for adults working in real estate. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

#Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For meaningful changes, we'll tell you by email or on the site before they take effect.
